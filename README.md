# Knock House Portfolio

Working demos and case studies of AI agents that handle real customer conversations and hand off cleanly to your team.

Knock House builds done-for-you customer-interaction agents (voice and chat) for appointment- and enquiry-driven businesses: qualifying enquiries, booking, follow-up and payment collection, with a clean hand-off to your staff.

> **Status:** this portfolio is being built in public. Every item below is labelled **Live**, **Demo** or **Planned**, so you always know what exists today.

## How an engagement works

1. **Discovery.** We map your customer journey from first contact to paid customer, including what happens when calls are answered and any recurring business.
2. **Proposal and scope.** We agree the scope, the hand-off points to your staff and how success is measured.
3. **Build and test.** We build the agent and test it with your team (UAT).
4. **Go-live and reporting.** Launch, then a monthly report and business review.
